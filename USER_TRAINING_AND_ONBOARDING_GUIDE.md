# Off-World Robot Architect: Visual Systems Onboarding & Training Guide
*A Step-by-Step Training Manual for Mission Specialists and Space Systems Interns*

## 🌠 Welcome to the Design Outpost, Mission Specialist!

Welcome, Intern! This walkthrough guide is designed to rapidly onboard you into the **Off-World Robot Architect** workbench. This training manual will ensure you can confidently navigate the dynamic controls of the interface, run operational stress tests, and translate complex technical blueprints into highly informative academic posters.

## 🎛️ Visual Walkthrough: The 5-Step Core Workflow

The user interface is structured into three main views, accessible via the technical tabs located just beneath the header bar:

```
+======================================================================+
|                  (Tab 1)               (Tab 2)             (Tab 3)   |
|               DESIGN BOARD           OPERATIONS            E-POSTER  |
+======================================================================+
```

---

### Step 1: Initializing a Custom Schematic Chassis

1. Locate the **Prototype Chassis Reference ID** box on the far left. Assign your vehicle a specific name (e.g., `Ares Glacial Retriever` or `Vanguard Bore Core`).
2. Toggle your target environment: **Moon** or **Mars**. 
   * *Visual Indicator*: Observe how the target environment box shifts color and updates its real-time temperature ranges in degrees Celsius. Look at the **System Diagnostic Schematic** container in the top right—swapping environments dynamically loads a stark gray surface background (Moon) or a deep rusty orange sandstorm horizon (Mars) behind your robot’s schematic frame!
3. Choose a baseline model from our preset deck in the header banner (e.g., *Ares-V*, *Artemis*, or *Ceres*). Doing so instantly pre-populates your CAD workbench with logical parameters and historical scientific design notes!

---

### Step 2: Customizing the Locomotive and Propulsion Loadouts

Move down the left sidebar to fine-tune your robot's key structural sub-grids:
1. **Robotic Archetype Category**: Select deep subterranean mining drills, surface surveying rovers, or inside habitat botanical assistants.
2. **Terrain Mobility Locomotion**: Choose titanium treads, multi-jointed spider crawler limbs, or rockerbogie tires.
3. **Primary Power Grid**: Swap standard regenerative solar arrays for plutonium-based Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs). 
   * *Restraining Physics Alert*: Note that choosing methane engines on the Moon turns red and locks down. The app's physical model warns you that the Moon lacks carbon-dioxide air pockets to operate gas engines!
4. **Human-Robot Teaming Protocol**: Determine whether astronauts control the machine via manual flight controllers, supervised delegation, or fully autonomous machine learning networks.

---

### Step 3: Payload Integration (Auxiliary Toolsets Only)

Look at the **Modular Payload Adaptations** panel next to the CAD diagram:
* Launch parameters enforce a **strict mass-weight budget** of a maximum of **3 active toolsets** at any time.
* Tap the technical buttons to toggle attachments (e.g., *Ground Penetrating Radar*, *Abrasive Ingress Brushes*, or *High-Frequency Plasma Welder*).
* *Dynamic Vector Check*: Look at the **Interactive CAD Diagram** above. As you toggle your active tools, watch the custom SVG component layers re-draw and colorize your robot's mechanical arms and sensors in real-time!

---

### Step 4: Running the Scientific SME Evaluation

Now, let's submit your completed design blueprint to the **Blue Marble Space SME Desk**:
1. Locate the **NASA Qualification Report** block.
2. Click **Submit Blueprint for Scientific Audit**.
3. *What happens*: If a valid `GEMINI_API_KEY` is loaded within the platform, the server triggers a deep, structured thermodynamic and physical analysis.
4. *Interpreting Results*: Your design launches an evaluation report scoring your schematic out of 100:
   * **Circular Score HUD**: Displays a dynamic, high-contrast circular score gauge. Teal indicators highlight optimal flight readiness, whereas amber or red warnings point to immediate mechanical freezeouts.
   * **Review Panels**: Expand the *Dust Resilience*, *Thermal Performance*, and *Teaming Synergy* details.
   * **Expert Recommendations**: Take note of the logical feedback returned inside the *Required Refurbishments* tracker list.

---

### Step 5: Troubleshooting with Dr. Vance (SME Chat)

Have questions about why your crawler tracks received a poor dust evaluation rating on Mars? Use our live chat:
1. Locate the **NASA SME Live Chat Desk** on the bottom right.
2. Type a specific engineering question (e.g., *"How do I protect my titanium joints from perchlorate dust on Mars?"* or *"What is the best power source for the 14-day lunar night?"*).
3. Tap Send. The virtual senior scientist, **Dr. Evelyn Vance**, receives your active robot design state and walks you through physical adjustments in real-time!

## 🌋 Phase II: Stress-Testing inside the Mission Operations Simulator

Want to see how your robot performs in actual disasters? Tap the **Mission Operations Simulator** sub-nav tab in your workbench:
1. Explore our library of planetary emergencies:
   * **Extreme Polar Frostbite Storm** (Critical Thermal Hazard)
   * **Lava Tube Cave-In** (Structural Collapse & Blockage Emergency)
   * **Lunar Dust Electrostatic Cyclone** (Particulate Ingress Hazard)
2. Tap **Initiate Operations Simulation**.
3. Review your emergency status reports. Step through each phase, making critical tactical decisions.
4. *Success Metrics*: Choosing tasks where your matching equipped payloads (such as using an *Electrostatic Brush* during dust storms) are active yields high point modifiers. Mismatched machinery docks points and forces dangerous emergency crew spacewalks.

---

## 🖨️ Step 3: Preparing Your Academic E-Poster Portfolio

Ready to show your academic work to classmates or advisors? Tap the **NASA E-Poster Blueprint** sub-nav tab:
1. Review the beautiful, clean outreach layout compiling your vector drawings, structural specifications lists, custom internship logs, and academic scores.
2. **AI Art Studio**: Optionally enter an artistic prompt or click **Generate AI Outreach Art**. Leveraging generative models, the application designs a photorealistic surface backdrop painting of your exact robot working on the surface!
3. Tap **Export / Print E-Poster** at the top right of the poster module.
4. This triggers your system print dialog, pre-formatted to export clean, full-bleed PDF portfolios or send them directly to local office printers.

## 💡 Specialized Engineering Tips for Mission Success

* **The Lunar Night Trap**: Do not use standard solar panels on the Moon! The 14-day lunar night will freeze your batteries. Swap your grid to **Radioisotope Plutonium Cores (RTGs)**.
* **Repelling Dust Storms**: Tracked systems can jam under sand loads on Mars. Mount **Active Dust Brush arrays** or choose **Rocker-Bogie wheels** to distribute weight cleanly across deep dunes.
* **Minimizing Astronaut Strain**: To scoring highest under Teaming Synergy indices, pair a **Collaborative Copilot AI personality** with **Supervised Field Teaming mode**. This creates an ergonomic workflow that lowers crew stress.
